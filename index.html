NBA Win-Probability Prediction Platform

Aug 2026
Modeling · Feature engineering · Production serving

An 11-season pregame win-probability pipeline with chronological splitting, a custom Elo engine, and a leakage-safe roster-continuity feature that proxies player injuries from game logs. Logistic regression was selected over CatBoost after evaluation. A walk-forward backtest across 8 season cutoffs and a live calibration monitor exposed a 1.7× calibration-error gap between back-to-back and rested games that the aggregate accuracy number completely hid.

Uncertainty-Aware Marketplace Pricing Analysis

Jul 2026
Applied statistics · Conformal prediction · Reliability auditing

A reproducible pricing pipeline over 426,880 Craigslist vehicle listings with 21-feature engineering, chronological splitting, and VIN-based leakage controls. I compared global median, hierarchical segment, Ridge, and CatBoost models, then layered split conformal prediction and subgroup reliability audits on top — which surfaced severe undercoverage on $50K+ vehicles that the headline coverage number averaged away.
