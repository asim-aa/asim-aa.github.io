02 · About

A bit about how I work

I'm a Mathematics–Computer Science major at UC San Diego, minoring in Cognitive Science, expecting my B.S. in March 2027. I transferred in from Las Positas College with an A.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science, and earned Provost Honors in Spring 2025.

Most of what I build starts from the same question: what does this model get wrong, and does it know it? That's shown up as calibration monitoring on an NBA win-probability model, conformal prediction intervals on a pricing pipeline, and fact-checking guards on LLM agent output, I'd rather ship a system that reports its own limits than one that just looks accurate on average.
